Island escape

Goa,
unhurried

Four slow days on the North Goa coast, planned around the forecast: beaches on the clear mornings, old Portuguese quarters and long lunches when the rain rolls in.

Duration
4 days
Budget
₹20,000
Best season
November to February
Stay
Beachside villa

The journey

Goa rewards a loose plan. We base you in a quiet villa a short walk from Calangute, so mornings can start on an empty beach and nothing on the itinerary is more than a short drive away.

Every day is checked against the live forecast before you arrive. The outdoor days sit on the clear ones, the indoor days are saved for showers, and any of them can be swapped with a message.

Good to know

Rain plan
If the forecast turns, day two swaps with day three: the forts wait for a clear day while the churches, markets and a cooking class fill the wet one.
Getting around
A driver is arranged for the forts and Old Goa. Beach days are on foot from the villa.

Day by day

The
itinerary

  1. Day 01

    Arrive and unwind

    Airport pickup, a late breakfast at the villa, and a first walk on Calangute beach at the end of the day. Dinner is grilled fish at a shack on the sand.

    Outdoor
  2. Day 02

    Forts and sunsets

    Fort Aguada and its lighthouse in the morning light, a swim at Sinquerim, then Chapora Fort at sunset for the view over Vagator.

    Outdoor
  3. Day 03

    Old Goa and Fontainhas

    A guided walk through the Basilica of Bom Jesus and the Latin Quarter of Panjim, with a long Goan lunch between. A good day to give to the rain if it comes.

    Mixed
  4. Day 04

    Spice farm and farewell

    A morning at a spice plantation near Ponda with lunch served on banana leaf, then a last swim before your evening transfer.

    Mixed
