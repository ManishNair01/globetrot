Mountain

Above the
clouds

Six days in a mountain chalet above the Lauterbrunnen valley, with big-sky hikes on the clear days and a warm fire, a fondue and a long book on the grey ones.

Duration
6 days
Budget
Set by you
Best season
June to September
Stay
Private chalet

The journey

The mountains make their own weather, so this is the trip where the forecast matters most. We hold every high-altitude day loosely and keep a valley alternative for each of them.

You stay in a private chalet with a kitchen and a view. The fridge is stocked before you arrive, and each morning we look at the forecast and tell you which day to spend up high.

Good to know

Rain plan
Each high day has a valley alternative. If cloud closes in, Schynige Platte and Mürren swap with Trümmelbach Falls and the cheese tasting, and the cable-car days move to the clear ones.
Season
Trains and lifts to the high viewpoints run from late spring to autumn, and some close for the winter.

Day by day

The
itinerary

  1. Day 01

    Arrival by train

    The scenic rail up to the valley, the chalet handover, and a first walk to the village for supplies. An early night.

    Outdoor
  2. Day 02

    The valley floor

    A flat walk past Staubbach Falls and through the meadows to Stechelberg. An easy day to find your legs.

    Outdoor
  3. Day 03

    Schynige Platte

    The cog railway to Schynige Platte and the ridge walk, with views over the Eiger, Mönch and Jungfrau. We pick this day for the clearest forecast.

    Outdoor
  4. Day 04

    The weather day

    Trümmelbach Falls, which run inside the mountain, a cheese tasting in the valley, and an afternoon by the chalet fire. It moves to whichever day turns grey.

    Mixed
  5. Day 05

    Mürren and Gimmelwald

    The cable car to Mürren and the high path to Gimmelwald, with lunch on a terrace facing the Jungfrau massif.

    Outdoor
  6. Day 06

    Last coffee, long train

    Breakfast on the balcony, the chalet handover, and the train down the valley.

    Mixed
