The journey

Santorini is beautiful, and busy. We stay you in Imerovigli, above the crowds of Oia but with the same caldera view, and plan the sightseeing for early morning and late afternoon.

The summer wind can stop the boats on some afternoons, so the sailing day is held against the forecast and moved if the sea gets up.

Good to know

Rain plan
Santorini is dry for most of the season. If the wind rises, the sailing day swaps with the winery visit and we move to a smaller boat or a drive along the coast.
Booking
Cliffside suites with a caldera view fill months ahead in July and August. The earlier you tell us the dates, the better the room.

Day by day

The
itinerary

  1. Day 01

    Arrive on the caldera rim

    Transfer to your cave suite, a swim in the plunge pool, and a first sunset from the terrace.

    Outdoor
  2. Day 02

    Fira to Oia on foot

    The cliff path from Fira through Firostefani and Imerovigli before the heat, ending with lunch and a late afternoon in Oia.

    Outdoor
  3. Day 03

    A day under sail

    A small catamaran around the caldera, with swims at the Red and White beaches, lunch on board, and a sunset return.

    Outdoor
  4. Day 04

    Wine and farewell

    A tasting at a family winery among the vines of Pyrgos and Megalochori, a final lunch, and your transfer.

    Mixed
