Three days across the Pink City: forts at first light, bazaars in the cool of the evening, and the hottest hours spent indoors where the palaces keep their courtyards shaded.

The journey

Jaipur is best seen early and late. We start your days before the crowds reach Amer Fort and finish them in the old city bazaars, leaving the middle of the day for shade, lunch and a rest.

You stay in a restored haveli inside the old city, so the walk to the City Palace and Hawa Mahal is short and the evenings stay quiet.

Good to know

Rain plan
Rain in Jaipur is brief but heavy. On a wet day the fort moves to the afternoon, and the workshop, a museum and a long lunch take the morning.
Heat
From March the afternoons get hot. We keep the middle of every day indoors and in the shade.

Day by day

The
itinerary

  1. Day 01

    Amer Fort at first light

    Beat the heat and the tour buses up to Amer Fort, then the stepwell at Panna Meena ka Kund. An afternoon rest at the haveli, and dinner on a rooftop facing Nahargarh.

    Outdoor
  2. Day 02

    Palaces and the old city

    The City Palace and Jantar Mantar in the morning, Hawa Mahal from a café across the street, and the Johari and Bapu bazaars once the sun drops.

    Mixed
  3. Day 03

    Block prints and a farewell thali

    A hands-on block-printing workshop in Sanganer, a long thali lunch, and your transfer to the airport or station.

    Indoor
