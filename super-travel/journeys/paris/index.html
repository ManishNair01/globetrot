City break

Paris,
slowly

A week in Paris with nothing hurried: one anchor a day at most, long lunches, and a boutique hotel on a street where you can hear the bakery open.

Duration
7 days
Budget
Set by you
Best season
April to June, September to October
Stay
Boutique hotel, Left Bank

The journey

Paris disappoints people who try to see all of it. We give you one anchor a day, a museum, a market or a neighbourhood, and leave the rest of the hours open for wandering.

The weather turns quickly, so each outdoor day has an indoor twin planned, a gallery or a covered passage, that we can swap in on the morning.

Good to know

Rain plan
Orsay or the covered passages stand in for the river walk, and the cooking class can move to any wet morning.
Budget
Paris is one of the pricier cities we plan. Give us your budget and we will tell you plainly whether a week fits it.

Day by day

The
itinerary

  1. Day 01

    Arrive in the Marais

    Check in, then a first walk through the Marais and the Place des Vosges. Dinner at a neighbourhood bistro, booked for you.

    Mixed
  2. Day 02

    The Louvre, early

    The Louvre on a timed entry before the crowds, lunch in the Palais-Royal gardens, and a quiet afternoon.

    Indoor
  3. Day 03

    Left Bank and Saint-Germain

    Bookshops, the Luxembourg Gardens and a long café afternoon in Saint-Germain, with a jazz club in the evening.

    Outdoor
  4. Day 04

    Markets and a cooking class

    A morning at the Marché d'Aligre, then a hands-on cooking class that turns the market haul into lunch.

    Indoor
  5. Day 05

    Montmartre

    Up the hill before the crowds, the vineyard and quiet streets behind the Place du Tertre, and the view from Sacré-Cœur at golden hour.

    Outdoor
  6. Day 06

    Orsay and the Seine

    The Musée d'Orsay in the morning, a river walk from the Pont des Arts to the Île de la Cité, and an evening boat on the Seine.

    Mixed
  7. Day 07

    A last croissant

    A slow breakfast, last-minute shopping in the covered passages, and your transfer to the airport.

    Mixed
